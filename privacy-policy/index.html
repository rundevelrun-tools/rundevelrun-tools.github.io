Privacy Policy

Transparency is at the core of our developer tools.

1. 100% Client-side Processing

At RUN:DEVEL:RUN, your privacy is our top priority. Unlike other online utilities, all our tools are built to run 100% within your web browser. No input data, images, or sensitive strings are ever transmitted to our servers or stored in any database.

2. No Personal Data Collection

We do not require user accounts, email addresses, or any personal information to use our tools. Since all processing happens on your local machine, we have zero access to the data you input or the results generated.

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings. We do not use any other tracking or analytics scripts that collect identifiable user data.

4. Open Source Transparency

To ensure complete trust, we encourage users to verify our implementation. You can find our source code on GitHub.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy or the security of our tools, please reach out via our GitHub repository.
